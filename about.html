About

From the help desk to running IT operations, in under four years.

I'm an IT professional with 4+ years of experience who grew from hands-on technician to IT Operations & Services Lead at ThinkTech IT, a New York managed service provider, through continuous upskilling in systems engineering, computer science and AI.

I've worked every layer of support. I serviced 100+ machines a day as a field technician, supported 100+ users at Puig, and joined ThinkTech as a Systems Engineer, where I consistently ranked #1 in weekly ticket closures. I was promoted to Team Lead and then, within 3 months, to IT Operations & Services Lead.

Along the way I built automations that turned manual, repetitive work into scalable processes, like a procurement order system that cut hardware fulfillment time by 40%. Today I lead a 10-person team and the company's largest client account with that same builder's mindset. Before IT, I ran STEM programs for 100+ students a day, which is still where my love of 3D printing, drones and robotics comes from.
