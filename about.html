About

From the help desk to running IT operations, in under four years.

I'm an IT Operations & Services Lead at ThinkTech IT, a New York managed service provider. I lead a team of 10 technicians and own the company's largest client account, working with our CTO and VP of IT Operations on operations strategy and the automation roadmap.

I got here by working every layer of support. I started as a field technician servicing 100+ machines a day, moved into enterprise support at Puig, then joined ThinkTech as a Systems Engineer handling Microsoft 365, Entra ID and Intune across 20+ client companies. I went from engineer to operations leadership in under two years.

What I enjoy most is fixing the system behind the tickets: redesigning Zendesk workflows, connecting tools through APIs, and building training that gets new technicians productive fast. Before IT, I ran STEM programs for 100+ students a day, which is still where my love of 3D printing, drones and robotics comes from.
